Thank you for visiting my website!

I am a Ph.D. candidate at University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC). I received my Master's degree at University of Illinois Chicago (UIC) in 2021 and Bachelor's degree at Xi'an Jiaotong University (XJTU) in 2020.

---
# Research Interests

My research interests lie at machine learning and large language models for chemistry and biology including:
* Computer-aided synthesis planning
* Reasoning LLMs and LLM agents for chemistry 
* AI for Science in chemistry and biology

| Please feel free to contact me for discussion and collaboration.
